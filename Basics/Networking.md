## Birth of networking

During 1950s cold war between USA and Russia era, Russia sent Sputnik to space and USA wanted to compete in science and technology. USA created a  ARPA (Advanced Research Projects Agency). To share details with each other they wanted a network then they created ARPANET. Although newtorks inside the instituions are able to share details with rules set by Network admin they had problem communicating to other networks.
- Then, a standardized protocol called the <strong>Transmission Control Protocol(TCP)</strong>

### Before Search Engine
Universities shared data using hyperlinks.. there is no search engine back then. Then, people started creating static indices of the web - a categorized listing of websites. People would sit and randomly click on links to find web pages and add links to their index. But this couldn’t scale. So, eventually, search engines were “invented.”
